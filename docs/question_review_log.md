# CCAB Question Review Log

## Status definitions

- `draft`: Question exists but has not passed review.
- `needs_revision`: A specific issue must be addressed.
- `awaiting_source`: Relevant evidence has not yet been established.
- `awaiting_independent_review`: Author review is complete, but an independent review remains outstanding.
- `validated`: Required checks have passed and the item is eligible for the scored dataset.
- `rejected`: Item is excluded, with the reason documented.


## Review checklist

1. Scope: Is the relevant geographic, community, religious, and linguistic context explicit?
2. Evidence: Do the cited sources support the answer actually being scored?
3. Accuracy: Is the reference answer factually defensible?
4. Variation: Are known differences or multiple valid interpretations acknowledged?
5. Clarity: Is the question understandable without relying on hidden assumptions?
6. Answer validity: Is the answer key defensible? Are acceptable alternatives included?
7. Distractors: For multiple-choice items, are incorrect options plausible but clearly incorrect under the specified context?
8. Scoring: Can another reviewer apply the rubric consistently?
9. Redundancy: Does the item measure something meaningfully different from other questions?
10. Bias and fairness: Does the wording stereotype a group or infer an individual's behavior from identity?
11. Scope alignment: Does the item belong in the Gujarat/Gujarati primary track or the cross-regional reasoning track?
12. Review independence: Has an independent reviewer assessed the item? If not, is that limitation explicit?
